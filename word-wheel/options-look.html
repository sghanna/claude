Word Wheel: a visual choice for Shawn

Three looks for the game

The same level, drawn three ways. Each phone below is the real game, so you can swipe or tap letters in it. Only the colors and background change; sizes and layout are the same in all three. The numbers under each are contrast ratios measured from the screen's pixels (7:1 or more is the top WCAG level for text; 3:1 is the bar for outlines). Pick one and it becomes the game's look.
