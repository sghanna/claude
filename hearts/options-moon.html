Claude Hearts: a visual choice for Shawn

Shooting the moon

Taking all 13 hearts and the queen of spades is the rarest win in a hand, so it gets its own night-sky moment. Tap Replay under a phone to see it again.

Option 1: my choice (live now)

Moonrise

The table dims to night, a big glowing full moon rises slowly (look for the heart-shaped crater), stars come out, and the words appear on a night-blue banner. One large shape moving slowly, so it stays calm and easy to follow.

Option 2: without it

The gold ribbon

Option 3: another way

Hearts to the moon

The same night sky and moon, but the 13 hearts and the queen of spades she collected fly up into it, showing what shooting the moon means. More shapes moving at once.
