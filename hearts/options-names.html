Claude Hearts: visual choices for Shawn

Name your opponents

Jump to your idea (section 4). New in the Menu: "Name your opponents". The first time, it asks for a promise to donate to a school in the player's neighborhood (any school they choose, on trust). After "I promise to donate", the three names can be changed on that phone. Only the names change; the game plays the same. Your picks (Sept 25) are live: up to 10 letters, and light boxes to type in.

What she will see

Live now, in order. These phones are samples: nothing typed here is saved.

Step 1

The Menu

The new button sits under Settings, above "Back to the game".

Step 2

The promise

Shown until she promises. "Not now" closes it and nothing changes.

Step 3

The names

A thank-you, then one box per player, labeled by where they sit. After this, the Menu opens the names directly.

Step 4

At the table

Her names on the name boxes, the status line and the big button, and in every result.

1. How long a name can be

Each name box is narrow (about 80 pixels on her phone). "Michael" and "Barbara" just fit at full size. A longer name has to get smaller, or be cut off.

Option 1: your pick (live now)

Up to 10 letters, longer ones shrink

Room for "Grandma Jo" or "Aunt Rosie". The reason: two-word names like these are likely. The cost: on her phone a 10-letter name drops from 18 to 13-15 pixels on the name box and under "Pass to" (12 on a smaller phone).

Option 2: without a limit

Any length

What the limit prevents. "Grandma Josephine" shrinks as far as it can and is still cut off on the name box.

Option 3: another way

Up to 7 letters, always full size

On her phone every name stays as big as "Michael" is today (18 pixels), easier for her to read. The cost: "Grandma Jo" won't fit; she would type "Grandma" or "Jo".

2. The boxes she types in

This is the game's first place to type. Tap a box in any phone to try it.

Option 1: my choice

Dark, like the buttons

Navy with a gold edge and big white letters (22 pixels, bold), so the boxes match the rest of the game.

Option 2: without it

The phone's plain box

What a web page gets by default: a thin grey edge and small, regular-weight letters (16 pixels). Hard for her to read.

Option 3: your pick (live now)

Light, like paper

The same size and gold edge, with dark letters on cream. It looks more like a form to fill in, and stands out from the buttons.

3. The box she is typing in

Added after your pick of light boxes: the edge I first made for the typing box turned pale gold, which is almost invisible on cream. She needs to see which name she is changing. Here the middle box is the one being typed in.

Option 1: my choice (live now)

Thick gold ring

A wide gold ring with a glow, the same look as the cards she can play, so gold keeps meaning "this one".

Option 2: without it

Pale gold edge

What the light boxes would have with my first version. The box she is typing in stands out less than the others.

Option 3: another way

Magenta ring

The same ring in magenta, the game's color for a card she has chosen. It stands out more against the gold edges of the other boxes.

4. Your idea: dark until she types

The boxes are dark like the buttons, and only the box she is typing in turns light. In each phone the middle box is the one being typed in. Tap another box to watch it change.

Option 1: your idea

Dark, the typing box turns light

As you described it: every box dark with a gold edge; the one she is typing in turns cream with dark letters. Nothing else changes.

Option 2: my recommendation

Your idea, plus the gold ring

The same, with the thick gold ring on the light box. The reason: two signals at once, a light box and a glow, and the ring is the same one the cards she can play get.

Option 3: live now

All light, gold ring

Your earlier pick: every box light, and the thick gold ring on the one she is typing in.
