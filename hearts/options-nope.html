Claude Hearts: visual and sound choices for Shawn

When she taps a card she can't play

Two things to choose: the sound, and how the shake and the hop you picked go together. Sound only plays if she turns Sound on in Settings and the phone isn't on silent; the two buzzes happen either way. Back to the first page.

1. The sound: six versions

Your pick, "uh-uh", is live now; you said the tone isn't quite right. The other five change the instrument or the shape. Try them on your phone too (this page works there): phone speakers drop low notes, so some sound quieter than on a Mac.

Option 1: your pick (live now)

Uh-uh

Two notes going down, with a buzzy, electronic tone.

Option 2: my recommendation

Marimba

The same two-notes-down shape, on a warm wooden bell. Gentler, and unlike any other sound in the game.

Option 3: another way

Nuh-nuh

Two short notes on the same pitch, soft and round.

Option 4: another way

Knock knock

Two soft knocks on wood. It is a little like the sound of a card landing.

Option 5: another way

Slide down

One note that slides down, like a small "aww".

Option 6: another way

Soft buzz

A short, muffled buzzer: the classic "wrong answer" sound, with the edge taken off.

2. The shake and the hop together

You picked both: the card she tapped shakes "no", and the cards she can play hop. Live now is my choice, one after the other. Tap a diamond in any phone, or use the button.

Option 1: my choice (live now)

In turn

The card shakes first, then the hearts hop, so her eye goes from "not this one" to "these ones". About 1 second.

Option 2: another way

At the same time

The shake and the hop happen together. Quicker (about 0.6 seconds), but two places move at once.

Option 3: another way

In turn, hop twice

Like Option 1, but the cards she can play hop twice, for a longer pointer. About 1.5 seconds.
