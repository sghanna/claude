Claude Hearts: a visual choice for Shawn

Which cards can she play?

The same moment in all three: spades were led, and her only spade is the jack, so it's the only card she can play. The game now shows Option 2 until you pick.

Option 1: my choice

Outline and a light dim

The gold outline around the playable row, plus a light dim on cards she can't play. The playable card stands out, and every rank stays readable for planning ahead.

Option 2: without it (live now)

Gold outline only

How it works today: a gold outline around the row she can play from. Every card keeps full brightness, but the choice is less obvious at a glance.

Option 3: another way

Strong dim, no outline

Cards she can't play turn noticeably darker, like most card apps. The clearest signal, but those ranks lose some contrast.
