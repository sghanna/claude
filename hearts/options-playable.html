Claude Hearts: a visual choice for Shawn

Which cards can she play? Checked against WCAG

WCAG 2.2 sets contrast minimums. Card ranks are large text, so the bar is 3:1 for AA and 4.5:1 for AAA. Signals such as an outline need 3:1 against the colors next to them (AA). WCAG excuses controls that are turned off, but she still reads the cards she can't play yet to plan ahead, so every option here is held to the same bar. All numbers were measured from the rendered screen. Same moment in all four: spades were led, and the jack is her only spade.

Option 1: your pick (live now)

Outline and light dim

Gold outline around the playable row. Cards she can't play dim to 72%. It passes AA everywhere, but red ranks on dimmed cards fall just short of AAA.

  • Red rank, dimmed: 4.3:1 (AA, just under AAA)
  • Black rank, dimmed: 9.8:1 (AAA)
  • Red rank, full: 5.4:1 (AAA)
  • Gold outline vs felt: 5.9:1 (passes)
Option 2: my recommendation

Outline and softer dim

The same, but dimmed to 80%. The smallest change that reaches AAA, and it keeps Solitaire's red. Dimmed cards look less different (1.6:1 instead of 2.0:1), so the outline does more of the work.

  • Red rank, dimmed: 4.7:1 (AAA)
  • Black rank, dimmed: 12.0:1 (AAA)
  • Red rank, full: 5.4:1 (AAA)
  • Gold outline vs felt: 5.9:1 (passes)
Option 3: most contrast

Outline, light dim, deeper red

Your pick with a deeper red ink on every card. Red ranks reach AAA even by the stricter small-text standard. The catch: red and black suits end up closer in brightness (2.6:1 instead of 3.5:1), so she tells them apart mostly by color. The red also no longer matches Solitaire.

  • Red rank, dimmed: 5.4:1 (AAA)
  • Black rank, dimmed: 9.8:1 (AAA)
  • Red rank, full: 7.2:1 (AAA, any text size)
  • Gold outline vs felt: 5.9:1 (passes)
Option 4: nothing dims

Gold ring on each playable card

No dimming at all. Each card she can play gets its own gold ring, and every rank keeps full contrast. The playable card is found by its ring alone, which is a weaker signal at a glance than dimming the rest.

  • Red rank, any card: 5.4:1 (AAA)
  • Black rank, any card: 19.0:1 (AAA)
  • Gold ring vs felt: 7.4:1 (passes)
  • Dimming: none

All four meet WCAG AA, and none of them depends on color alone: the outline or ring is a shape, and dimming changes brightness. Dimming on its own would not be enough (the dimmed card face is only 1.6-2.0:1 from a bright one), which is why every dimming option keeps the gold outline.
