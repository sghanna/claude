Ace and queen: six versions

Option 4 from the icon page, drawn six ways. Most make the cards bigger than the icon so they run off the edges; that makes the A, the Q and the heart larger at Home Screen size. Under each icon, switch the magenta selection glow (on the ace, as when she chooses a card in the game) and a brass border around the whole icon on or off. Each is shown next to her Solitaire icon at real size.
