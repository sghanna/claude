Claude Hearts: a visual choice for Shawn

How should a good moment feel?

Three moments get a celebration: a clean hand (no points, small and short), shooting the moon, and winning the game (both bigger). The results box waits until the celebration ends. Pick a moment under each phone to replay it. Without any celebration, the results box appears right away, as it did before today.

Option 1: my choice (live now)

Gold ribbon

A large gold banner with a few twinkling stars. The fewest moving shapes, so the words stay easy to read. About 2 seconds for a clean hand, 4 for the big moments.

Option 2: another way

Shower of heart cards

The same banner, with small heart cards floating up the screen. Livelier and more playful, but many shapes move at once.

Option 3: family motif

Sky lanterns

Red and gold lanterns rise slowly, echoing the Solitaire win. They carry the same nine characters as agy-solitaire's lanterns; that set mixes traditional and simplified forms, which is worth a family check.
