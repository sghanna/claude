Claude Hearts: visual choices for Shawn

After her playtest

Four choices from what you saw while she played, plus one sound. Your picks (Sept 24) are marked and live now. The phones are the real game at iPhone size; you can tap them. Next: six not-allowed sounds, and how the shake and hop go together.

Already fixed and live (no choice needed)

  • Long taps count. Holding a finger down on a card or button no longer selects text or opens a menu, and it still counts as a tap when she lifts. Text can't be selected anywhere in the game, dialogs included.
  • A tap that slides still counts. If her finger starts on the Play button (or any button or card) and slides up to about a finger's width before lifting, it still counts as a tap on that button.
  • A buzz for "not allowed". Tapping a card she can't play gives two short buzzes. It works with the phone on silent. This uses an iPhone trick (iOS 18 or later), so check it on her phone; it can't be tested from a Mac.

1. Cards she can't play

Her moment: hearts were led, she has hearts and diamonds. Both are red, and right now the diamonds are only dimmed a little, so red sits next to red. Contrast was measured from the rendered screen (WCAG AAA for large text is 4.5:1).

Option 1: my recommendation

Grey out the cards she can't play

The same dimming as now, plus all color removed. The diamonds turn grey, so the only red cards left are the ones she can play. The gold outline stays.

  • Greyed diamond rank: 6.8:1 (AAA)
  • Greyed black rank: 9.4:1 (AAA)
  • Playable red rank: 7.0:1 (AAA)
Option 2: your pick (live now)

Light dim and gold outline

Cards she can't play dim to 72%. The diamonds keep their red, which is the mix-up you saw.

  • Dimmed diamond rank: 5.3:1 (AAA)
  • Dimmed black rank: 9.5:1 (AAA)
  • Playable red rank: 7.0:1 (AAA)
Option 3: another way

Fade them into the table

Cards she can't play turn see-through, so the green table shows through them. The biggest difference at a glance, but the faded ranks are harder to read when she plans ahead.

  • Faded diamond rank: 3.3:1 (AA only)
  • Faded black rank: 4.5:1 (AAA, just)
  • Playable red rank: 7.0:1 (AAA)

2. When she taps one anyway

Today the line above her hand turns gold and says why ("You must follow hearts."). The buzz above now comes with it on every option. Tap a diamond in any phone, or use the button to tap the 8 of diamonds in all three at once. All three use today's light dim so only this changes.

Option 1: your pick, with Option 3

The card shakes "no"

The card she tapped shakes side to side, like a head saying no, and the gold message appears.

Option 2: before

Message only

Nothing moves; only the gold message appears.

Option 3: your pick, with Option 1 (hops twice, live now)

The cards she can play hop

The card she tapped stays still; the cards she can play hop up once, to show where to look.

3. The passed cards go into her hand

After someone passes her 3 cards, she taps Continue. Each phone taps Continue by itself a moment after it loads.

Option 1: your pick (live now)

They glide into her hand

The 3 cards travel from the table down into their places in her hand, a moment apart. Each place stays empty until its card lands. About 0.9 seconds in all.

Option 2: before

They just disappear

The cards vanish from the table. The new cards are already in her hand, but nothing shows her where they went.

Option 3: another way

One at a time, slower

Each card makes the trip on its own and the next one waits until it lands, so her eye can follow every card. About 2.6 seconds in all.

4. Top of the screen: points, logo, buttons

Each phone has switches for the three things you named, so you can combine them. To make the "+3" bigger without covering the names (today's badges already cover part of "Michael" and "Jerry"), the bigger badges sit above the name plates, which moves the table down 12 to 18 pixels and makes her hand cards slightly shorter. With the logo, "Hearts" gets smaller to fit between the buttons; in Spanish and Vietnamese the word drops out and the logo stays. Taking off the border doesn't free up room for the title, since the buttons stay the same size. Your pick (live now): logo on, borders off, biggest points.

5. The "not allowed" sound

Sound is off in her Settings, and even when it's on, the iPhone's silent switch mutes it. That's why the buzz above matters more. If she turns sound on, this is what she'd hear when she taps a card she can't play.

Option 1: your first pick; Marimba is live now

Two notes going down, like "uh-uh". Easier to hear on a phone speaker.

Option 2: before

One soft, low note. Phone speakers barely play low notes.
